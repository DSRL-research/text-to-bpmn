If goods shall be shipped, the secretary clarifies who will do the shipping. In parallel, the goods can be already packaged by the warehousemen. If you have large amounts, special shipping will be necessary. In these cases the secretary invites three logistic companies to make offers and she selects one of them. In case of small amounts, normal post shipment is used. In the same time, a package label is written by the secretary, and a parcel insurance is taken by the logistics department head if necessary. If everything is ready and merged, the packaged goods are prepared for being picked up by the logistic company.
