**Read with:**

- **BoostRelEx**: its stored predictions are not those of the published run (overall 0.790 against Tab. 1b's 0.82; CatBoost training is not deterministic). The published Flow F1 is 0.79 (Fig. 7c), with no P or R.
- **What each system saw of PET**: BoostRelEx was trained on the other four folds; a k-shot LLM row had the k most similar other PET documents, with their gold relations, as examples; RuleRelEx's rules were written for PET. The method has no PET example in its prompt, but its relation stage was revised after looking at its errors on PET (provenance/relation_stage_2026-11-11.md), so PET is partly its development set.
- **The four rules** were fixed before the first score; *rule 1 only* shows what they account for. They write the model's own structure in PET's conventions (a condition between a decision and its branch, an else-marker heading the other branch) and add nothing the model does not say.
- **Folds** for the LLM rows partition their pooled run by CoopIS 2023's folds; ER 2024 reported pooled scores. The method, the text order and the LLMs used no training split, so their two columns differ only in the averaging.
